Twitch stream tool

DJ Gloggi

DJ Gloggi is a music request bot for my Twitch stream. Viewers type !dj with a YouTube link in chat, and the track is added to a playlist that plays during the broadcast.

How it works

  • A viewer posts a YouTube link in chat.
  • The bot checks the video's length and availability.
  • If it passes, the video is added to a YouTube playlist owned by the streamer.
  • An on-screen overlay shows what's playing and who requested it.

Who uses it

This app is used by one person — me, the streamer — to manage my own YouTube playlist. It is not offered as a service to anyone else and does not access any viewer's Google account.
